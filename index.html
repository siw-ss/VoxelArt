VoxelArt

A collection of interactive voxel scenes with AI 🤖 by Siwar Soula

🏯

Cherry Blossom Pagoda Garden

A floating voxel island suspended in a pastel sky — home to a five-tier pagoda with teal roofs and gold eaves, blossoming sakura, a koi pond with stone lanterns, and 900 drifting cherry petals.

Interactive Scene
🏝️

Floating Archipelago

Five disconnected voxel islands suspended at different altitudes in an endless sky, connected by luminescent crystal bridges with cascading waterfalls, ancient ruins, and flocks of chickens and bright yellow chicks roaming between islands.

Interactive Scene
🏔️

Nordic Ice Palace

A crystalline ice palace on a floating island surrounded by a frozen lake, with animated aurora borealis overhead, drifting snow particles, and time-of-day presets that transition the sky between green night, midnight blue, and purple dusk.

Interactive Scene
🏮

Lantern Lake

A dreamy sunset lake inspired by the lantern scene from Tangled — warm golden water reflecting hundreds of glowing sky lanterns, a small wooden boat drifting at the center, surrounded by willow trees, fireflies, and a magical twilight gradient sky.

Interactive Scene
🌋

Volcanic Crater Complex

An active volcanic island with molten lava flowing through obsidian channels, steam vents erupting from sulfur-stained chimneys, crystalline mineral deposits glowing among basalt columns, and ash particles drifting beneath a smoldering sky.

Interactive Scene
🐠

Underwater Coral Kingdom

A vibrant reef on rippled sand where bioluminescent pods glow beside swaying kelp forests, schools of voxel fish circle sunken temple ruins, and bubbles rise through shafts of filtered sunlight — with a depth slider that descends into the abyss.

Interactive Scene
